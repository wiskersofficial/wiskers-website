How it works

Scan. Explore. Be remembered.

Illustrative example

  1. 01

    They scan.

    A code on your stall opens your experience on their phone straight away. No app to install, nothing to sign up for.

  2. 02

    They explore.

    A short, tappable experience made for a phone. Visitors choose what to look at, in the order they like, at their own pace, so your brand comes across the way you mean it to.

  3. 03

    You learn.

    Every choice becomes a signal. When a visitor decides to share their details, with their consent recorded, you leave the event knowing who was interested and in what.

What you learn

A badge scan says they stopped by.
A Wiskers says why.

What they chose

Every tap is a decision. See which problems, products and ideas people picked, and which they passed over.

How far they went

Interest is more than a yes or a no. See what people came back to, how deep they went and where they moved on.

Who they are, if they say

Visitors can leave their name and contact details, and say whether you may follow up. Their consent is recorded with every contact.

What to say next

Follow up about the exact thing that caught their attention, not a generic “great to meet you”.
