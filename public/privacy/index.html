Privacy and site data

Your data, explained.

A short, practical summary of what the Wiskers website does today.

Privacy notice in progress. This page covers the site features currently visible in its source. The legal operator, hosting logs and retention details still need confirmation before this can serve as a complete formal privacy notice.

What this website stores

If you choose Day, Night or Twilight, the site stores that preference in your browser’s local storage under wiskers-theme so it can remember your choice on a later visit. You can remove the saved choice here or through your browser’s site-data settings.

Some pages use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. Clarity is optional: it loads only after you choose “Accept analytics”. If you reject, Clarity is not loaded. If you accept, Clarity may collect interaction data and session recordings and use first- and third-party analytics cookies, including _clck and _clsk. See Microsoft’s Clarity cookie list.

Your choice is stored in this browser for six months under wiskers-analytics-consent. You can change it here or clear it through your browser’s site-data settings.

The site has no account sign-in, contact form or advertising pixel.

Fonts and external services

Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts, including DM Sans. Those requests go to Google and may include your IP address and standard browser request information. See Google’s privacy information.

The interactive cat model is served as part of this website. The footer links to the model’s creator and its Creative Commons licence.

If you email us

The contact link opens your email application. If you choose to send a message to hello@wiskers.click, we receive the information in that message and use it to respond. The email provider, retention period and any later transfer into a customer system still need to be confirmed.

Questions or a privacy request

For a question or request about information you have sent us, email hello@wiskers.click. The legal entity responsible for the website, its postal contact details and hosting provider will be added when confirmed.
