Market and alternatives

Our initial market is B2B marketing teams that commission campaigns for products and services. The first narrower customer profile, use case and reachable market size remain to be established; no market-size figure is assumed.

Agencies & existing campaigns

Teams can commission creative agencies, conventional landing pages and lead magnets.

Interactive builders

Tools already offer branching quizzes and experiences, including involve.me.

Ad formats & analytics

LinkedIn carousels and TikTok playable ads provide existing interactive formats. Analytics can already record custom events.

Our proposed advantage is a reusable experience engine and library supporting bespoke experiences and observable interactions. That advantage remains a hypothesis: delivery time and cost, useful signals, customer outcomes and repeat demand need to be demonstrated.

Product links describe provider capabilities, not independent performance results or Wiskers traction.

Commercial model and paid pilot

A campaign fee covers an agreed creation and launch scope; an ongoing service can cover distribution management, engagement reporting and improvement. Pricing, delivery costs, hosting, media buying and media spend are agreed per campaign and not yet set.

A paid pilot is intended to test whether Wiskers can deliver a useful experience, whether its interaction signals inform a decision, and whether the production economics and customer response support repeat work. The success measures remain to be agreed.

Evidence, not assumptions

Client campaigns need evidence of delivery quality, economics and repeat demand. Bagel needs a reader cohort, return use and reader value. The two applications share the engine but have separate evidence paths.

Funding and runway are plans to validate against founder pay, employer costs, compute, delivery, growth, overhead, revenue and any credits. Numeric targets, customer pricing and the first buyer profile are not yet agreed.
