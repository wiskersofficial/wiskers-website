Four ways to
open a conversation.

Each corner describes a useful job an experience could do. Your point can sit between them: visitors often need more than one kind of help.

01

Help me choose

You want to meet people while they’re ready to move, even if they’re still deciding what they need. Make the next step feel clear and welcoming.

A guided chooser that helps a visitor name what they need, then points them to a useful next step.

02

Help me act

You’re drawn to people who know the problem and are ready to do something about it. Help them act without adding friction.

A short action planner that turns a clear need into a concrete enquiry or next step.

03

Spark my interest

You want to catch attention early and make exploring feel worthwhile. A little discovery can give a visitor a reason to stay.

A playful reveal that helps visitors discover which of your offers fits them.

04

Help me understand

You want to help interested people understand their options before they commit. Give them something useful to compare and consider.

An interactive comparison that shows visitors which option best fits their situation.

Where it fits.

Interactive experiences can help prospects understand an offer before they meet your team. The experience can take different forms depending on what they need to explore.

✳

Introduce a new offer

Give prospects a clear first look at what you offer, who it is for, and how it could fit their business.

Explain a complex offer

Make a layered product, service, or approach easier to explore, so prospects can focus on what matters to their situation.

Help prospects compare options

Show how different products, service levels, or approaches differ, and let prospects explore what each means for them.
