# Wiskers website preview

The user's cat direction from 15 October 2026 applies to future work here:

- The user decides the cat's path and actions. Implement only the beats they describe; do not restore old animation plans or invent new scenes.
- The current agreed cat scene starts seated on “content” with an upward gaze. Three quiet idle poses play over a short scroll; runtime grooming has been removed. As the cat approaches the top of the screen, it reacts, falls back-first, rights itself in midair, and lands in the lower area of the blue “Insight takes input” tile. The tile rocks slightly counterclockwise at impact and settles. The cat stays attached inside the tile afterward. Do not add other cat actions or scenes.
- `public/art/cat-master-c/reference.png` is the durable, byte-for-byte copy of the user's permanent C visual master for every cat pose and animation. Derive every newly generated cat atlas from it, preserving the ginger tabby, blue eyes whenever open, white markings, face and body proportions, rounded playful pixel style, and fixed rendered character size. Do not switch visual masters between poses or generations. Its source and SHA-256 are recorded in `public/art/cat-master-c/MASTER.txt`.
- Only Luna agents may use image generation for new cat art or refinements. Reuse suitable existing C-derived art, and describe requested frames precisely. Keep the user's character and pose choices authoritative.
- Current scene atlases are `public/art/cat-idle-c-v3/atlas.json`, `public/art/cat-fall-c-v1/atlas.json`, and `public/art/cat-transition-c-v2/atlas.json`. Their common viewport is 128 × 128 with a foot anchor at (64,113); the visible seated character is approximately 78 × 85.5 CSS pixels at every breakpoint. Each frame uses its `cellRects` to clip to its source grid cell and a normalized `viewBox` to preserve the same visible size and anchor. The renderer keeps a persistent SVG image layer per atlas and decodes every sheet before revealing the cat; it completes a frame's crop and viewBox before switching layers. Keep the actual visible character size consistent, not merely the wrapper size. The older grooming atlas remains approved archive material; `public/art/cat-content-c-v1/` and `public/art/cat-content-v1/` are also archived. Do not use archived art as a style master or delete it.
- Inspect all frames and their transitions before integrating. New art is optional; consistency is mandatory.
- Keep mobile scroll-driven and controls unobstructed. During the fall, move the blue tile and all following content upward more slowly than natural scrolling, then keep the resulting offset while normal scroll resumes. Never intercept scroll, pin or snap the page, reverse content movement, or pause scrolling. Reduced motion keeps the cat seated on “content” and disables the fall, parallax and impact.

This folder is the local redesign source. Follow the standing dev-to-main release authorization and validation workflow below; do not publish unrelated work.

## Version control direction, 15 October 2026

The user explicitly authorized committing and pushing every completed website change to `dev`, then merging the checked change into `main`. `main` is connected to the public Cloudflare release workflow. Keep changes in small, traceable commits, push `dev` promptly after each finished change, and merge/push `main` after appropriate validation. Preserve remote work; do not force-push or discard unrelated changes. The active preview now has the website repository history and `origin` at `https://github.com/wiskersofficial/wiskers-website.git`.

Keep the insight tile at its normal content-driven size. Create the falling room with continuous scroll motion, not extra internal card padding. The temporary cat frame counter is visible in the local dev server and available on deployed pages with `?cat-debug=1`. It pairs sequential IDs 01–03 for idle and 04–27 for the interleaved fall with stable pose labels such as `fall-v1-06`, `twist-03`, and `land-04`. The README's current revision records the crosswalk from old fall-only counter numbers.
