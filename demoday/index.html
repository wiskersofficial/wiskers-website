The problem

A form. A download.
Then what?

Teams selling complex B2B offers pay for attention, then gate it behind a form.

In B2B, a common approach is to offer a guide, report or video in exchange for contact details. The buyer gets an asset to take away, and the business gets a lead. What if discovering the offer was an experience in itself?
